# Public Experience and Presidential Validation Specification

**Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Status:** Product and validation requirement  
**Applies to:** Human Metadata Engine / True Human Design

## 1. Product Principle

The engine exists to make the data valid. The published artifact exists to make the valid data meaningful, beautiful, memorable, and alive.

Most people do not want to inspect raw astronomical positions, hashes, topology tables, evidence weights, or machine-readable ledgers. Those must remain available for audit, export, challenge, and expert review, but they should not dominate the primary experience.

The default public experience should feel like entering a coherent symbolic world built specifically around the subject:

- visually immersive;
- mythic without fabricating history;
- emotionally resonant without manipulating the user;
- clear enough to understand;
- layered enough to revisit;
- transparent about uncertainty;
- capable of accepting contradiction and feedback.

The user should encounter the meaning first and the proof beneath it.

## 2. Layered Publication Model

Every publishable artifact has four layers.

### Layer 1 — The Experience

The default reading surface presents:

- a strong visual identity;
- a concise central archetype or pattern;
- names, lineage, symbolism, and chart themes woven into a coherent narrative;
- interactive or expandable sections;
- meaningful language rather than raw field names;
- a tone appropriate to the subject and the project.

This layer may be magical, poetic, mythic, or spiritually evocative.

It must not invent factual genealogy, certainty, causation, or validation that the underlying engine has not earned.

### Layer 2 — The Interpretation

The interpretation layer separates:

- traditional interpretation;
- user-research interpretation;
- Inversion interpretation;
- cultural and mythological resonance;
- contradictions and unresolved questions.

Interpretation may synthesize across domains, but each domain retains provenance.

### Layer 3 — The Evidence

The evidence layer explains why the engine made each claim:

- observed behavior;
- self-report;
- measurable structure;
- etymology;
- symbolic systems;
- source confidence;
- contradictory evidence;
- alternative explanations.

This layer should be accessible through “Why this appeared,” “Show the evidence,” or equivalent controls.

### Layer 4 — The Ledger

The complete ledger remains available for export and expert inspection:

- normalized inputs;
- source records;
- astronomical calculations;
- rule-set versions;
- active and rejected rules;
- hashes;
- boundary distances;
- confidence dimensions;
- challenge history;
- raw JSON.

The ledger is not the default reading experience.

## 3. Public Feedback Disclaimer

Once the engine has passed its release gates, every public reading includes a short invitation rather than a defensive legal wall.

Required public wording:

> This reading is generated from a continuously tested symbolic engine. Take what resonates, question what does not, and report meaningful mismatches. Your feedback helps us identify patterns, exceptions, and errors that cannot be discovered from calculation alone. Nothing here outranks your lived experience.

A secondary expandable note may state:

> Resonance is not proof, and disagreement is not failure. We compare your feedback against the calculation, source quality, competing explanations, and other consented cases. Reports may refine an interpretation, expose an outlier, reveal missing context, or falsify a rule.

The interface must make reporting easy:

- `Resonates strongly`
- `Partly resonates`
- `Does not resonate`
- `Factually incorrect`
- `Needs context`
- free-text explanation;
- optional permission to retain the feedback for research;
- optional follow-up consent.

Feedback must attach to a specific claim or section whenever possible.

## 4. Feedback Is Evidence, Not a Popularity Vote

The engine must not optimize toward maximum agreement or flattering output.

Feedback is classified by:

- claim ID;
- subject ID;
- calculation version;
- interpretation version;
- response category;
- explanation;
- whether the feedback describes internal experience, observable behavior, factual correction, or preference;
- confidence;
- consent;
- timestamp.

A high agreement rate does not prove a rule. A low agreement rate does not automatically falsify it.

The refinement system must test whether mismatches arise from:

- calculation error;
- bad or incomplete input;
- source error;
- interpretation error;
- wording failure;
- cultural mismatch;
- genuine outlier expression;
- temporal change;
- generic language;
- unsupported rule;
- insufficient evidence.

No rule is changed solely to increase resonance scores.

## 5. Historical Recursion Cohort: Pre-Nixon Presidents

### 5.1 Scope

The initial historical recursion cohort consists of every U.S. president before Richard Nixon:

- George Washington through Lyndon B. Johnson;
- 36 presidencies;
- 35 unique individuals because Grover Cleveland served nonconsecutive terms.

Nixon is excluded from the initial cohort boundary. His administration may later serve as a temporal out-of-sample test.

### 5.2 Why This Cohort

These subjects provide unusually dense public records across multiple centuries:

- official papers;
- speeches;
- letters;
- diaries;
- biographies;
- cabinet and staff accounts;
- policy decisions;
- elections;
- military and diplomatic actions;
- health and family records;
- oral histories;
- recordings and secret tapes for later administrations;
- documented successes, failures, contradictions, and changes over time.

The cohort is not representative of humanity. It is deliberately narrow: male, politically selected, historically powerful, culturally constrained, and heavily mythologized.

Its value is depth, not representativeness.

### 5.3 Source Hierarchy

Presidential observations use the following source order:

1. primary documents written or spoken by the subject;
2. contemporaneous records and official papers;
3. private correspondence, diaries, recordings, and verified transcripts;
4. direct witnesses with clear provenance;
5. scholarly biographies using cited primary sources;
6. later synthesis and historical consensus;
7. popular anecdotes, mythology, partisan narratives, and unsourced quotations.

Lower tiers may guide investigation but cannot independently validate a behavioral claim.

Core repositories include:

- The American Presidency Project;
- Miller Center presidential biographies, speeches, oral histories, and tapes;
- National Archives presidential libraries;
- Library of Congress manuscript and presidential collections;
- published presidential papers and archival editions.

### 5.4 Birth-Data Quality

Historical depth does not guarantee exact birth time.

Every subject receives a birth-data quality grade:

- `AA` — birth record or equivalent primary documentation;
- `A` — direct personal or family record with strong provenance;
- `B` — sourced biography or contemporaneous record;
- `C` — indirect or uncertain time;
- `DD` — conflicting or speculative time;
- `X` — date known, time unknown.

Rules:

- AA/A/B data may enter time-sensitive Human Design validation;
- C data may enter sensitivity testing but not clean golden vectors;
- DD data remain contradiction cases only;
- X data may test date-based, name-based, and biography-based layers but not houses, Ascendant, profile boundaries, or other time-dependent claims;
- rectified times are never silently treated as observed birth times.

### 5.5 Blind Validation Procedure

For each president:

1. freeze the sourced birth record and provenance;
2. calculate the chart without reading the behavioral dossier;
3. hash and preserve the calculation ledger;
4. generate preregistered claims from the active rule set;
5. construct a behavioral dossier from sourced records;
6. reserve holdout periods, decisions, or documents;
7. score each claim against supporting and contradicting evidence;
8. compare against alternative Types, Authorities, Profiles, and null descriptions;
9. record every mismatch;
10. prohibit single-subject tuning;
11. repeat using competing rule sets;
12. publish aggregate results only after the scoring criteria are frozen.

### 5.6 Behavioral Domains

The presidential cohort may test:

- initiation versus response;
- decision latency and process;
- relationship to advice and authority;
- informing, secrecy, and resistance;
- sustained versus burst-based work patterns;
- conflict response;
- public versus private persona;
- crisis behavior;
- leadership structure;
- repeated failure modes;
- adaptation across life stages;
- differences between campaign behavior and governing behavior;
- differences between authored language and staff-produced language.

The engine must distinguish the person from the office, institution, speechwriter, party, and historical constraints.

### 5.7 Mythology Control

Presidents are among the most mythologized historical subjects.

The validation corpus therefore separates:

- documented behavior;
- self-presentation;
- campaign construction;
- official image;
- partisan interpretation;
- posthumous reputation;
- later national mythology.

A famous quote or reputation does not become a behavioral fact until its provenance is verified.

### 5.8 Temporal Holdouts

The corpus should use chronological holdouts where possible.

Examples:

- develop interpretation from pre-presidential life, test against presidency;
- develop from first term, test against later term;
- develop from public record, test against private letters or tapes;
- develop from peaceful periods, test against crisis decisions;
- train on presidents before a historical cutoff, test on later pre-Nixon presidents.

This reduces retrospective fitting.

### 5.9 Negative Controls

Required controls include:

- shuffled charts;
- shuffled names;
- alternative Type descriptions;
- generic high-resonance descriptions;
- biographies with identity removed;
- staff-written speeches versus personally written material;
- date-only calculations where time is unavailable;
- multiple plausible birth times for disputed cases.

A claimed signal must outperform these controls.

## 6. Cohort Output

Each historical subject produces two artifacts.

### Research artifact

- complete ledger;
- source provenance;
- confidence grades;
- preregistered predictions;
- evidence for and against;
- holdout score;
- negative-control performance;
- unresolved contradictions;
- rule-set comparison.

### Public artifact

- beautiful, coherent narrative experience;
- clearly marked historical and mythic layers;
- concise confidence and source notes;
- optional “show the proof” controls;
- feedback invitation;
- no raw data wall by default.

## 7. Release Gate for Public Readings

A public magical experience may ship only when:

1. calculation invariants pass;
2. source provenance is complete;
3. known boundary cases are surfaced;
4. interpretation rules survive the anchor case and historical cohort tests;
5. negative controls are implemented;
6. resonance feedback cannot silently rewrite rules;
7. every claim has a stable claim ID;
8. the public disclaimer and report controls are present;
9. users can report factual and experiential mismatches;
10. private feedback is stored only with explicit consent;
11. the audit ledger remains exportable;
12. unresolved contradictions are not hidden by the presentation.

## 8. Success Condition

The product succeeds when a nontechnical person can experience the reading as beautiful, magical, and personally meaningful while an engineer or researcher can open the same artifact and verify exactly how every claim was produced.

The experience may cross the veil. The evidence must still leave footprints.
