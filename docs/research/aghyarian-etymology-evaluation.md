# Aghyarian Etymology Evaluation

## Decision

The engine accepts **Aghyarian** and **Aghiarian** as documented spelling variants and analyzes the surname as an Armenian patronymic built from an **Agha** title-root plus the Armenian **-ian** suffix.

Safe gloss:

> **family or descendants of Agha**

This is a morphological interpretation, not proof that a specific ancestor held office, owned land, belonged to nobility, or used the title formally.

## Evidence retained

### Armenian surname structure

FamilySearch describes Armenian surnames as a root plus one or more suffixes. It defines **-ian/-yan** and related forms as meaning “issued from” or “family of,” and notes that roots can derive from titles, occupations, personal characteristics, given names, or places.

Source: `https://www.familysearch.org/en/blog/your-armenian-surname`

### Aghyarian / Aghiarian variants

C. K. Garabed’s *Dictionary of Armenian Surnames* lists AGHIARian and AGHYARian as spelling variants. This is useful secondary evidence, but FamilySearch explicitly notes that the dictionary is a community-compiled project and that its author does not claim to be a linguist or philologist. The engine therefore records this source as **community-compiled secondary**, not definitive linguistic authority.

Source: `https://www.armeniapedia.org/wiki/Dictionary_of_Armenian_Surnames_A`

### Agha root

*Agha* is a Turkic/Ottoman honorific carrying meanings such as master, lord, chief, respected man, or influential person. Historically it could be used for civilian or military officers, court personnel, local leaders, or influential landholders.

Source: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agha_(title)`

## Claims retained with qualifications

- The surname belongs to an Armenian naming tradition shaped partly by Ottoman-era titles and multilingual transliteration.
- Aghyarian and Aghiarian may represent the same surname under different Latin transliterations.
- Searches should preserve both variants and later add the native Armenian spelling when documentary evidence identifies it.
- Similar-looking Agha-derived surnames may share a lexical root without sharing a recent genealogical line.

## Claims not promoted to fact

### Exact U.S. bearer count

The claim that approximately 32 people in the United States bear the surname was not supported by the cited FamilySearch article and could not be independently verified from an authoritative population dataset. It is excluded.

### Automatic geographic origin

Research on an Aghjayan family in Palu, Kayseri, or another region is not evidence that an Aghyarian line came from the same locality. Related-surname case studies may guide research methods but cannot assign an ancestral village.

### Treating all similar surnames as one family

Aghajanian, Aghjayan, Aghjian, Aghayan, and other visually similar forms are not automatically variants of Aghyarian. Some dictionary entries give materially different roots or meanings. The engine will not merge them without documentary or genetic evidence.

### Genetic conclusions from a surname

Population-genetic research supports substantial Armenian continuity after Bronze Age admixture, but a surname cannot establish an individual’s genomic composition or prove uninterrupted descent from the Armenian Highlands. Claims such as a fixed Neolithic-European percentage or three millennia of ancestry cannot be attached to a person from surname evidence alone.

Relevant paper: Marc Haber et al., “Genetic evidence for an origin of the Armenians from Bronze Age mixing of multiple populations,” *European Journal of Human Genetics* 24 (2016), 931–936, DOI `10.1038/ejhg.2015.206`.

## Engine rules

1. Preserve the user-supplied spelling exactly.
2. Store canonical variants separately from identity replacement.
3. Keep lived surname, genetic lineage surname, chosen names, and creation names as different relationship types.
4. Etymology may inform historical meaning but receives no personality or genetic inference weight.
5. Native-script spelling, ancestral locality, and family linkage remain unresolved until sourced.
