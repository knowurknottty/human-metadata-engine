# Literary Expansion Contract

Status: canonical narrative-growth rule for The Human Manual.

Every successive narrative-focused agent or cohort must materially increase the deterministic literary depth and breadth of the project.

## Growth gate

- **2× minimum**: the accepted literary inventory after a narrative pass must be at least twice the accepted post-review inventory that existed before that pass.
- **5× preferred**: the preferred target is five times the prior accepted inventory.
- The denominator is always the **accepted post-review inventory**, not a historical baseline and not raw generated output.
- A pass that only critiques prose without contributing usable literary material does not satisfy this contract.

## What counts as a literary unit

A unit may be a distinct phrase, sentence form, transition grammar, metaphor family, cadence pattern, tonal-register transform, system-specific vocabulary element, anti-repetition structure, or other deterministic compositional primitive.

A unit counts only when it is semantically distinct, deterministic, composable, provenance-safe, and suitable for actual integration into the Human Manual prose engine.
## What does not count

Padding, synonym spam, trivial grammatical rewrites, duplicated metaphors, raw model output that is never integrated, and ornamental verbosity do not count toward the multiplier.

No literary expansion may weaken evidence boundaries, historical provenance, privacy guarantees, deterministic replay, accessibility, or the distinction between calculation and interpretation.

## Required evidence per agent/cohort

Each narrative-focused pass must record the prior accepted-unit count, 2× minimum target, 5× preferred target, proposed-unit count, accepted-unit count, rejected-unit count, and rejection reasons.

The next agent inherits the accepted post-review inventory as its baseline. Growth therefore compounds only through material that survives review.

Current v1 baseline: **90 accepted units** across eight deterministic banks. The next qualifying pass therefore targets **180 minimum / 450 preferred** accepted units.
