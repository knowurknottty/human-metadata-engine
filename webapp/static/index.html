The Human Manual,

Humans have spent thousands of years trying to describe humans — through names, numbers, stars, cycles, archetypes, psychology, ritual, and systems of meaning. This tool computes those maps separately, shows where they converge or disagree, and keeps every layer traceable to its method.

No single system gets to define you. The point is to see the maps, the evidence, the limits, and the patterns side by side — then decide what is useful yourself.
